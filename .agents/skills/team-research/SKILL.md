# Research: The Recon Pod

Execute the [shared team procedure](../subagent-protocol/references/team-execution.md) once per run. It supplies capability briefs, host/provider inheritance, ownership, review budgets, evidence, recovery, and optional pane/Kiro adapters. Delegation must already be authorized.

## Roles and ownership

| Role | Profile / native alias | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Analyst | `analyst` (`arcwright-analyst`) | Research evidence and source index |
| PM | `pm` (`arcwright-pm`) | Product implications and recommendations |
| Writer | `tech-writer` (`arcwright-tech-writer`) | Final reference document |

## Stages

1. Analyst researches the scoped questions and focus areas, retaining sources, dates, uncertainty, and evidence behind conclusions. A second Analyst is optional only for a substantial independent research track within the authorized budget and live capacity; assign disjoint questions and report paths.
2. PM synthesizes findings into key insights, strategic implications, recommendations, and open questions. Distinguish established facts from inference rather than strengthening weak evidence through repetition.
3. Writer structures the research and brief into an accessible reference: overview, findings, analysis, recommendations, source/artifact index. Coordinator checks the delivered artifacts and resolves factual gaps with the owning role within the shared budget.

## Completion

Deliver a sourced, usable research package addressing the brief, with uncertainty and decision implications visible. No production implementation, tracker synchronization, external messaging, or publication is implied by research. Keep temporary notes in the run area and permanent requested reports in their designated location.
